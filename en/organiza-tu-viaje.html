Information for travelers

  • Documents and bookings: for the Inca Trail and the train to Machu Picchu, you will need a valid ID card or passport at the time of booking and travel.
  • Altitude and weather: Cusco sits at 3,400 meters (11,150 ft) above sea level. We recommend arriving one or two days early to acclimatize before demanding tours or high-altitude treks.
  • Questions about your booking: if you have questions about payments or your itinerary, contact us on WhatsApp and an advisor will help you directly.
