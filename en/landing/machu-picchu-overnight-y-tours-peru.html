Machu Picchu Overnight 2-day/1-night tour from Cusco with hotel included

My Cusco Trip's Machu Picchu Overnight experience is arranged across two consecutive days. On day one, travel from Cusco to Machu Picchu Pueblo, also known as Aguas Calientes, by tourist train and spend the night at the 3-star Hotel Luz Garden with breakfast included.

On day two, take the guided Machu Picchu visit on your chosen available circuit, use the official Consettur buses and return toward Cusco by train. Circuit availability is checked for the actual admission date—the second day of the program.

Keep the included trains or upgrade to The Prime and an earlier return, add the Tinkuy buffet at Belmond Sanctuary Lodge, and combine the experience with other tours in Cusco or Lima. Everything is organized under one booking and one secure PayPal payment.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the Machu Picchu Overnight tour?

It includes transfers from Cusco, round-trip tourist train, one night at the 3-star Hotel Luz Garden with breakfast, official admission, Consettur buses, a professional guide and local support.

Which date should I select?

Select the start date. The system automatically occupies that day and the following day because the program lasts 2 days / 1 night.

Which day do I visit Machu Picchu?

The guided visit and Machu Picchu admission take place on day two. Availability for Circuits 1, 2 and 3 is checked for that date.

Which outbound train times are used?

The base operation uses The Voyager departures at 4:30 a.m. or 6:40 a.m. on day one. Your selected return train departs Machu Picchu Pueblo on day two.

Can I upgrade my trains or return earlier?

Yes. You can select The Prime for the outbound journey, the 7:00 p.m. early return, or both upgrades. The total updates before payment.

Is lunch included?

Lunch is not automatically included. Continue without lunch or add the Tinkuy buffet at Belmond Sanctuary Lodge for USD 71.90 per paying traveler.

Can I add other tours?

Yes. Add experiences in Cusco or Lima. The calendar marks both Overnight days as occupied and prevents another tour from being booked on either date.

Can I use a discount coupon?

Yes. Enter a valid code in the booking summary before continuing to payment.
