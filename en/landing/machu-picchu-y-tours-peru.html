Frequently asked questions

Do I have to buy accommodation to book Machu Picchu?

No. This booking is for tours only. You can start with Machu Picchu Full Day and add Humantay Lake, Rainbow Mountain, the Sacred Valley, an Ancestral Welcome, the Seven Lakes of Ausangate, the South Valley, or Paracas–Ica–Huacachina. A hotel is neither included nor required.

Can I choose a different date for each tour?

Yes. Each tour you add has its own date selector, independent of the others.

Can I combine Machu Picchu with Paracas, Ica and Huacachina?

Yes, but because one tour departs from Cusco and the other from Lima, you need at least one free day to travel between the two destinations. The system checks this automatically when you choose your dates.

Which tours can I add during my days in Cusco?

You can add Humantay Lake, Vinicunca Rainbow Mountain, the Sacred Valley of the Incas, an Ancestral Welcome, the Seven Lakes of Ausangate, or the South Valley. Each experience has its own date and per-person price.

Can I organize the tours around my flights and hotel nights?

Yes. This option lets you fit each experience into the days you already have available without requiring accommodation. Our team will review transfer times and operating feasibility before issuing the services.

Are meals included in Machu Picchu Full Day?

Not automatically. You can continue without lunch or add the Tinkuy buffet at Belmond Sanctuary Lodge for USD 71.90 per paying traveler.

When are admission, trains and schedules confirmed?

Our team coordinates official admission, trains and schedules before issuing the services. If the selected option cannot be confirmed, you may choose an alternative or receive a full refund.

Can I apply a discount coupon?

Yes. Enter a valid discount code in your booking summary before continuing.
