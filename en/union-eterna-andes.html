What is the ceremony like?

We define the intention

We learn about the couple's story, the reason for the celebration, the date and the style they want.

We prepare the symbols

We coordinate offerings, symbolic rings, attire, music and ceremonial elements according to the package.

We celebrate the union

The ceremonial guide leads the encounter with respect, explaining the meaning of each moment.

We share and give thanks

The experience ends with a moment of gratitude and, in the complete version, a small gathering with guests.

Cultural respect: this proposal avoids presenting Andean spirituality as a spectacle. The exact content may vary depending on the ceremonial guide, the community, the authorized location and the conditions on the day.
